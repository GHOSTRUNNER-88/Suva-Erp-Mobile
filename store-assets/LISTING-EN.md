## Full description — 3,439 / 4,000

```
Suva ERP puts your whole business on your phone — sales, purchases, stock,
payments and double-entry accounting, in Nepali and English, in Bikram Sambat
and Gregorian dates.

It connects to your Suva ERP account, so the numbers on your phone are the
same numbers your accountant sees on the desktop. Nothing to enter twice.

BILL FROM ANYWHERE
• VAT invoices, sales orders, quotations and delivery challans
• Purchase bills, purchase orders, credit notes and debit notes
• Payment in and payment out, cheques, cash transfers and expenses
• Share any invoice as a PDF to WhatsApp, email or your printer — rendered
  from the same template your office print uses
• Send a payment reminder to a customer in one tap

PHOTOGRAPH A BILL, GET A FILLED FORM
Point the camera at a purchase bill, invoice, expense receipt, payment
voucher, credit note, debit note or cheque. Suva reads it, matches the
supplier and the items against your own catalogue, and clearly flags anything
it isn't confident about. Nothing is saved until you confirm it.

REAL NEPALI, NOT A TRANSLATION LAYER
Over 1,100 strings translated on the phone and 4,500 across the platform,
with English and Nepali at full parity. Switch language from any screen. In
Nepali mode, figures render in Devanagari numerals, currency becomes रु, and
chart labels count in हजार, लाख and करोड.

BIKRAM SAMBAT THROUGHOUT
Every date field opens a BS calendar with real Nepali month grids and
bilingual month names. Every report takes a BS date range. The same
conversion engine runs on the phone and on the web, so a date can never
drift between the two.

STOCK YOU CAN TRUST
• Live stock per item, per warehouse, with low-stock flags
• Stock adjustments and warehouse transfers from the floor
• Barcode and QR scanning — QR, EAN-13, EAN-8, Code 128, Code 39, UPC-A and
  UPC-E — with torch, haptic feedback and a manual code fallback

THIRTEEN REPORTS IN YOUR POCKET
Trial balance, profit & loss, balance sheet, VAT summary, day book, sales
register, purchase register, customer ageing, supplier ageing, inventory
summary, low stock, bank statement and sales summary — each with a BS or AD
date range.

PARTIES AND LEDGERS
Customer and supplier profiles with a full running ledger: opening balance,
period movement and closing balance, filterable by type and date. Party
statements, dues and party groups.

PAYROLL ON THE PHONE
Employee directory, day-by-day attendance with present, absent, leave,
half-day and overtime, monthly salary runs, salary letters and payroll
reports.

WORKS WHERE THE SIGNAL DOESN'T
Download your company data across 26 record types and keep working. Changes
you make offline are queued and sent automatically when you reconnect, with
an offline banner so you always know which mode you're in.

SECURE BY DEFAULT
Sign in with email and password or with Google. Lock the app behind Face ID,
Touch ID or your fingerprint. Access follows the roles and per-action
permissions set on your Suva account — staff only see what they should.

MULTI-COMPANY
Belong to more than one organization? Switch between them from the app,
without signing out.

MADE FOR BUSINESSES IN NEPAL
Tax invoices in the IRD layout, VAT at 13% computed from posted ledger lines,
sales and purchase registers in the statutory annex forms, and fiscal years
that run Shrawan to Ashadh — not January to December.

An active Suva ERP account is required. Start a 15-day free trial at
suvacorp.com.np.
```

## Data safety & content rating — answer these honestly before submitting

The app collects and transmits: email address, name, user IDs (Firebase),
device push tokens, camera images (uploaded for OCR), and all the business
data the user enters. It uses Firebase Auth, Firebase Cloud Messaging, Expo
push, and AWS Textract for OCR. Declare each of these in the Data safety
form, and make sure your published privacy policy URL covers them — Play
rejects listings where Data safety and the policy disagree.

The camera and biometric permissions both need a plain-language justification
in the listing or the policy; `app.json` already carries
`NSFaceIDUsageDescription` for iOS, but Play asks separately.
