# SUVA ERP — v1.0.0

First release. SUVA ERP on your phone, connected live to your SUVA ERP desktop account — same data, same numbers, no separate bookkeeping.

**What's in this release:**

- **Sales & Purchases** — invoices, orders, quotations, and delivery challans, with credit/debit notes for returns and corrections
- **Parties** — manage customers and vendors, grouped and organized
- **Inventory** — items, stock adjustments, and transfers between locations, with barcode scanning
- **Payments & Banking** — payments, cash transfers, cheques, and bank accounts in one place
- **Payroll** — employees, attendance, salary letters, and payroll reports
- **Expenses & Reports** — track spending and pull business reports on the go
- **Nepali + English** — every screen in both languages, switch anytime
- **BS + AD dates** — Nepali (Bikram Sambat) and English (Gregorian) calendars throughout
- **Secure sign-in** — Face ID / fingerprint unlock

Everything stays in sync with the desktop app — there's nothing to double-enter.
